# 🌱 MAP Onboarding Journey: Crossing the Threshold into Sovereign Participation
_Designing an onboarding experience that supports a Successful Customer Outcome (SCO): full, enthusiastic engagement with the MAP ecosystem._

---

## 🎯 SCO: What Does Success Look Like?

> _“I have joined the MAP in a way that feels safe, aligned, and meaningful. I now have a sovereign identity, control over my visibility and participation, and access to spaces aligned with my purpose.”_

This onboarding journey is designed to deliver that outcome — not as a compliance chore, but as a welcoming **membrane-crossing experience** that initiates a new relationship with the commons.

---

## 🌀 1. **Invitation from the Outside World**

The journey begins when Jordan — a regenerative storyteller — sees a **video teaser** shared on Instagram from the Memetic Activation Series.  
It resonates deeply. The link says:

> _“Watch the full video on the MAP. Join a community of regenerative storytellers.”_

Clicking the link launches the **MAP Onboarding Flow**, because:
- Jordan is not yet a MAP participant.
- No content inside any MAP Space is visible without onboarding.

---

## 🔐 2. **Membrane Gate: Create Your MAP Identity**

**Screen 1: Welcome to the MAP**
> _“The Memetic Activation Platform supports sovereign identity, meaningful collaboration, and aligned discovery. To participate, you’ll need a MAP identity — your key to a trusted, regenerative ecosystem.”_

**Prompt:**
- Choose a display name and MAP handle.
- Accept MAP’s core principles: sovereignty, consent-based participation, and trust-first design.
- Confirm understanding: “Nothing is visible until you join a Space.”

🎯 *Outcome:* A new **Agent I‑Space** is created. Jordan now exists on the MAP.

---

## 🧬 3. **LifeCode Initialization**

**Screen 2: What Moves You?**
> _“Your LifeCode is the energetic fingerprint of your purpose, values, and resonance. It guides who you find, and who finds you.”_

**Prompt:**
- Select values (e.g., `#Interbeing`, `#Justice`, `#EarthWisdom`)
- Choose 1–2 memes or movements you resonate with (e.g., `#Bioregionalism`, `#CulturalHealing`)
- Set visibility preference:
    - Publicly discoverable
    - Discoverable only within joined spaces
    - Private unless explicitly shared

🎯 *Outcome:* A draft **LifeCode holon** is formed in her I‑Space, ready to evolve.

---

## 🏡 4. **Join Your First Connection Space**

**Screen 3: Choose Where to Begin**
> _“Spaces are the membranes of the MAP. You must be inside one to see or share anything.”_

**Prompt:**
- Recommended Spaces based on LifeCode:
    - `Planetary Space` (default, high-reach, low-trust)
    - `Regenerative Culture Space`
    - `New Members Welcome Space`
- Join with observer role, or accept an invitation if one was extended

🎯 *Outcome:* Jordan joins the **Planetary Space**. Content becomes visible. DAHN activates.

---

## 🧭 5. **Welcome Tour via DAHN**

Now that she's inside a Space, Jordan is greeted by the **DAHN visualizer**:
- Shows her I‑Space
- Highlights new memes she’s aligned with
- Offers a soft introduction to:
    - Recent Weaves
    - Invitations
    - Popular content matching her LifeCode

The tour includes:
- How to refine her LifeCode
- How to respond to a Promise
- How to issue her own Offer or Enquiry

🎯 *Outcome:* Jordan understands the landscape, sees what’s relevant, and feels oriented.

---

## 🎁 6. **Optional: Activate Sense**

**Screen 4: Stay Connected on Your Terms**
> _“Want to receive updates outside the MAP? Sense adapters let you listen (or speak) through channels you choose.”_

**Prompt:**
- Choose inbound Sense feeds (email, WhatsApp, BlueSky, etc.)
- Optionally subscribe to Memetic Activation updates
- Configure outbound sharing preferences (teasers only, links to onboarding, etc.)

🎯 *Outcome:* Sense profile initialized. Jordan can participate in hybrid flows.

---

## 🌟 7. **Celebrate the Crossing**

Final screen:
> _“You’re now inside. You have a sovereign presence on the MAP. You can make and receive promises, join spaces, and activate what matters most.”_

**Optional suggestions:**
- Watch your first full video
- Join an orientation circle
- Add a visual or quote to your I‑Space front door
- Leave a gratitude note for your inviter

🎯 *Outcome:* Psychological closure. Jordan feels like she belongs.

---

## 🎯 Final SCO Recap

Jordan exits onboarding with:

- A **sovereign presence**
- **Visibility** into one or more aligned Spaces
- A **LifeCode beacon** guiding future matches
- Confidence and **readiness to activate**
- An **open door** to discovery, contribution, and community

> Onboarding is not a gate. It’s a rite of passage — a threshold crossed with clarity, consent, and celebration.
