### 🗺️ *Putting Yourself on the MAP*
#### Orienting, Expressing, and Coordinating as a Sovereign Agent

---

### 1. **Orientation: Locating Yourself in a Complex World**

“Putting yourself on the MAP” is literal and figurative.

Just as you locate yourself on Earth using physical coordinate systems (GPS, watersheds, ecozones), MAP supports **multiple coordinate systems**:
- **Spatial**: where your device is
- **Temporal**: your timezone or cycle
- **Cultural**: your values, principles, and LifeCode

These coordinate systems are **stewarded by commons** — bioregional, cultural, or scientific communities — and bridged into the MAP via adapters or APIs.

> 🧭 *By situating yourself within these systems, you begin to see your place — and your role — in the living fabric of the MAP.*

---

### 2. **Sovereignty by Default: Your I-Space**

When you join the MAP, you receive a private **I-Space** — a digital space analogous to your body or home.

It holds your:
- **LifeCode**: your values and memetic signature
- **Data Grove**: your personal knowledge and experience
- **Trust Channels**: the gates through which interaction happens

Like your nervous system, **sub-agents** (software processes, DAHN visualizers) act on your behalf — without requiring your constant attention.

> 🧠 *The MAP respects your salience landscape: what matters rises to awareness; everything else just works beneath the surface.*

Your data lives **on your devices**, under your control — not scattered across corporate clouds.

---

### 3. **Agents on Your Side: A Different Kind of Digital Ally**

In today’s digital systems, most agents (apps, ads, bots) serve someone else’s agenda.

In the MAP, agents act **on your behalf**.

They negotiate, remember, protect, and express your intent — bound by promises you control.

You can **delegate**, not just automate — and your sub-agents are accountable to your LifeCode.

> 🤖 *What if your phone respected your priorities more than advertisers’ incentives?*

---

### 4. **DAHN: Your Portal into the MAP**

DAHN is not an app. It's a **Dynamic Adaptive Holon Navigator**.

It shapes your experience of the MAP:
- Presents choices that match your vibe
- Shows your data in ways that make sense to you
- Reflects your aesthetic, language, and cultural context

It is **yours** — a sovereign interface curated by you and informed by collective use.

> 🎨 *DAHN doesn’t tell you what to see. It helps you see what matters — your way.*

---

### 5. **From Signals to Flow: Making Promises, Building Weaves**

Every commitment in MAP is a **Promise** — a consent-based declaration of intent.

Your I-Space can:
- Offer services
- Make contributions
- Participate in governance

When multiple Promises align, they form **Promise Weaves**, the scaffolds of collaboration.

> 🌐 *From individual cells to collective organs, Weaves become the tissue of trust across the MAP.*

---

### 6. **Discover and Connect: Through Trust Channels**

You can connect your I-Space to the broader world by:
- Plugging in existing channels (email, Discord, Mastodon, etc.)
- Authenticating with your device — your keys, your terms
- Filtering what gets through, how it's presented, and when you respond

The **Notification Center** becomes a buffer — a salience shaper — protecting your attention while honoring your intent.

> 🔔 *It’s not about cutting off the world — it’s about inviting in only what serves your flow.*

---

### 7. **Beyond Devices: Stewarded Infrastructure for All**

What if you don't have a phone or computer?

- MAP allows **device networks** to be shared or gifted
- **Commons-based clouds** make digital life accessible, without surveillance or lock-in

You can co-steward infrastructure with others — or benefit from **gift clouds** hosted by aligned communities.

> ☁️ *The cloud becomes a commons. The network becomes an ecosystem.*

---

### 8. **Where Is This Going? Growing Your Presence on the MAP**

Putting yourself on the MAP includes:
- Cultivating your LifeCode
- Growing your I-Space and connecting to others
- Expressing your values through Promises
- Coordinating through Agent Spaces
- Visualizing alignment through DAHN
- Participating in governance as a peer
- Navigating vital capital flows that regenerate life

And all of this happens **under your control**, **on your terms**, and **at your pace**.
