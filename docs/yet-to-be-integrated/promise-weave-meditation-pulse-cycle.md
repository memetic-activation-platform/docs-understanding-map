## A Guided Meditation -- Pulsing your Enquiry into the Field


Let’s begin by settling into the body.

Take a slow, deep breath in through the nose,  
and a long, gentle breath out through the mouth.

Again.

Breathing into the body.  
Letting the mind soften.  
Allowing the inner voice to quiet, just a little.

There’s nothing to figure out right now.  
Nothing to optimize.

Just notice.

---



## The Pulse 

Now hold the Enquiry paper in your imagination. Perhaps first take one final look...

- the LifeCode commitments,
- the gifts you’re willing to offer,
- the needs you’re asking to be met.

Imagine creating a copy of only the top-half of the paper (LC + N, but not O)

Imagine placing that copy into a small capsule.  
An envelope.  
A seed.

On your next **out-breath**, imagine gently pulsing this capsule outward into a shared field.

A connection space.

You are not broadcasting yourself.

You are offering an enquiry.

---

## Breathe In -- Receiving Responses

On your next **in-breath**, imagine receiving responses.

Not from everyone,  
but only from those with genuine resonance.

Those who:

- share enough of your LifeCode commitments to honor them, and
- have gifts to offer that meet at least some of your needs.

As each response arrives, notice what it contains:

- Which of your needs they can meet.
- Which of your LifeCode commitments they can honor.
- And also what they are asking for in return:
    - their needs,
    - their LifeCode commitments.

Take this in slowly.

No pressure to agree.  
No obligation to respond.

Just information.

---

## Pause (breath naturally) — Reflection

Now comes an important moment.

Notice the needs you named that were **not met**.  
Notice the LifeCode commitments that others did not share.

Gently ask yourself:

- How important is this need?
- Is it essential, or could it be relaxed?
- Is this LifeCode commitment something I truly require in collaborators?

And now notice what others are asking of you.

Ask yourself honestly:

- Can I authentically promise this?
- Would committing to this deepen my integrity, or compromise it?
- Is this something I am willing to grow into?

Only you can answer these questions.

No system can decide this for you.

Take a few breaths here.

---

## Refinement and Re-Pulsing

If you choose, imagine refining your enquiry:

- relaxing a need,
- deepening a LifeCode commitment,
- or offering an additional gift you hadn’t named before.

Now imagine gathering this revised enquiry into a new capsule.

On your next **out-breath**, pulse it again into the field.

On the **in-breath**, receive what comes back.

Notice how the field responds differently.  
Notice how you are changing as well.

This is **co-sensing**.  
This is **co-evolving**.

---

## Convergence and Agreement

Now imagine a moment where everything aligns:

- All the needs you are asking for are met.
- All the needs being asked of you, you are willing to meet.
- The LifeCode commitments are shared.

You are at the threshold of an agreement.

Perhaps agreement requires one more step:

- meeting face to face,
- seeing one another’s eyes,
- visiting a place together.

Whatever is needed — imagine it happening.

And then imagine this moment:

All parties freely commit.  
Promises are made explicit.  
An agreement is formed.

It is shared.  
It is signed.  
It is held by all — unchanged unless consciously revised.

This is the culmination of the Promise Weave.

---

## Closing

Take one more slow breath.

Notice what you’ve learned:

- about yourself,
- about what matters,
- about what you’re willing to promise.

When you’re ready, gently return your awareness to the room.
